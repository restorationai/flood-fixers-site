## What Usually Causes a Washing Machine to Flood a House?

A burst rubber supply hose is the most common cause, not a broken machine. The hoses that connect the hot and cold valves to the back of the washer are often the original factory hoses, and rubber degrades with age and water pressure until it splits, sometimes while no one is home.

A few other failure points show up often in San Marcos homes. A drain hose can slip out of the standpipe during a spin cycle and send gray water across the floor instead of down the drain. A supply line fitting that was never fully tightened after an install, a move, or an appliance swap can work loose over months and leak slowly before it lets go all at once. If you're due for a hose replacement, many plumbers recommend swapping rubber hoses for braided stainless steel lines, since they hold up far longer under constant pressure.

## Do I Call a Plumber or a Water Damage Company First?

Call a plumber only if water is still actively running from a fitting or valve you can't shut off yourself. For everything else, a water damage restoration company handles the extraction, the drying, and the documentation your insurance adjuster will ask for.

The water itself matters here. Supply line water that hasn't touched the floor yet is Category 1, clean water, under the [ANSI/IICRC S500 standard](https://iicrc.org/s500/). But water that comes out during the wash or drain cycle, carrying detergent, lint, and fabric residue, is Category 2 gray water, and it usually means wet carpet pad, laminate underlayment, or baseboards get removed rather than just dried in place. Clean supply water that sits on a floor for more than a day or two can also degrade toward Category 2 as it picks up contaminants from the building materials around it.

## What If the Laundry Room Is Upstairs and Water Came Through the Ceiling?

A stained or sagging ceiling below an upstairs laundry room means the subfloor and insulation above it are already saturated, even if the drywall still looks mostly dry on the surface. Two-story floor plans are common in San Marcos neighborhoods like San Elijo Hills and near Cal State San Marcos, and a laundry room on the second floor puts the washer directly over finished living space.

Don't wait for a stain to spread before acting. Insulation soaked with gray water loses its R-value and can hold moisture against the drywall for days, which is exactly the setup mold needs. A [ceiling water damage repair](/services/ceiling-water-damage-repair/) job in this situation usually means cutting an access section, pulling wet insulation, drying the joist bay with targeted air movement, and then patching and repainting once moisture readings come back to a dry baseline.

## What Happens When You Call a Restoration Company for a Washing Machine Flood?

The call itself takes a few minutes and ends with a technician dispatched, often able to start work the same day depending on the time and your location in San Diego County. Have your insurance policy information handy, note which valve you shut off, and snap a few photos of the standing water and the washer connection before you move anything.

On arrival, a technician checks moisture levels in the flooring, baseboards, and any ceiling below with a moisture meter, then sets up [emergency water removal](/services/emergency-water-removal/) equipment: extraction wands for standing water, air movers to push dry air across wet surfaces, and a dehumidifier sized to the room. If flooring or drywall is too saturated to dry in place, those materials come out before the drying equipment goes in. Everything gets documented, moisture readings, photos, equipment logs, because that paperwork is what your insurer will want to see.
