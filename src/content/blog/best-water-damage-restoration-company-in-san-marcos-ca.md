## What Should You Actually Check Before Hiring One?

The fastest way to separate a solid water damage company from a risky one is to confirm three things before a technician ever sets foot inside: IICRC certification, proof of license and insurance, and whether the company will document the loss for your insurance adjuster.

IICRC certification means the technicians are trained to the [ANSI/IICRC S500 standard](https://iicrc.org/s500/) for water damage restoration, which covers how to categorize the water, set drying equipment, and verify a structure is actually dry before closing out the job. A company that skips documentation (moisture readings, photos, equipment logs) can leave you exposed if the insurer disputes the claim later.
