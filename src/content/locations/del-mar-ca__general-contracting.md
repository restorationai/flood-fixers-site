Del Mar's building stock sits in an unusual spot: a small coastal footprint squeezed between bluffs, lagoon wetlands, and some of the highest property values in San Diego County. Renovations, remodels and general contracting here rarely mean a simple gut-and-refinish. More often it's a post-damage rebuild after a roof leak finally reveals rot in a 1960s beach cottage, or a kitchen remodel that has to work around a coastal permit review before a single wall comes down. Flood Fixers handles both the construction and the paperwork that comes with building this close to the ocean.

## Why Del Mar Properties Need a Different Rebuild Approach

Salt air corrosion is the quiet problem in Del Mar. Fasteners, HVAC coils, and exterior-grade fixtures wear out faster here than they would a few miles inland, and homeowners often don't connect a slow leak or a failing window seal to years of marine air exposure until a remodel opens the wall and the damage is visible. Add to that a housing stock that mixes original mid-century cottages near the bluffs with newer custom builds, and a contractor has to know when original materials can be matched and when they genuinely can't. Properties closer to the lagoon and low-lying areas near San Dieguito also see periodic flood risk, which affects what we're allowed to rebuild with below base flood elevation.

## Our Renovations and General Contracting Process in Del Mar

We start every project with a damage and scope assessment, whether the job originated from a water or fire loss or is a planned remodel. For post-damage rebuilds, that means documenting the original condition for the insurance claim before demolition begins. From there we handle structural repairs, framing, drywall, flooring, and finish work, coordinating kitchen and bathroom remodels around any plumbing or electrical that needed replacement due to the original damage. Because Del Mar sits in the California Coastal Zone, projects that touch exterior walls, roofline, or square footage near the bluff can require coastal development review before permitting, and we build that lead time into the schedule rather than letting it surprise a homeowner mid-project.

## Del Mar Insurance and Permit Coordination

Most of our rebuild work here starts as an insurance claim: a pipe burst damaged the subfloor, or a roof failure during a winter storm took out a ceiling and the drywall beneath it. We document the loss, work from the adjuster's scope where one exists, and handle the permit applications through the City of Del Mar's building department. Coastal properties sometimes need an additional layer of review depending on location relative to the bluff edge or wetland buffer, and we flag that early so the insurance timeline and the permit timeline don't end up working against each other.

## Local note

One detail that catches homeowners off guard: a kitchen or bathroom remodel in an older Del Mar beach cottage often needs the electrical panel upgraded before new appliances or fixtures can go in, because many of these homes were wired decades before modern code requirements and were never updated when later owners added square footage. We check panel capacity during the initial walkthrough so it doesn't become a mid-project delay.
