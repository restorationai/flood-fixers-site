## Why Del Dios Properties See Water Damage Issues

Much of Del Dios is semi-rural, with properties spread across hillside and canyon terrain rather than tight suburban lots. Homes built on sloped ground are more prone to grading and drainage problems during San Diego County's intense winter rain events, when runoff that would normally sheet across a flat yard instead pools against a foundation or finds its way under a crawlspace. Add in well-fed plumbing systems, which run at different pressures than city water and can stress aging galvanized or polybutylene pipe, and you get a higher rate of slow leaks that go unnoticed until flooring or drywall is already saturated.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Del Dios

We start with extraction, pulling standing water from floors, crawlspaces, and subfloor cavities before moisture has a chance to migrate further into framing. On rural and hillside lots, that often means positioning truck-mounted extraction equipment where driveway grade and access allow, which we scout before the crew arrives rather than after. Once the bulk water is gone, we map moisture with meters and thermal imaging to find out how far it traveled under flooring and behind baseboards, then set air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the structure, not a generic formula. Structural drying in homes with well water intrusion also gets a closer look at mineral staining and scale buildup, since hard water deposits can mask how saturated a material actually is.
