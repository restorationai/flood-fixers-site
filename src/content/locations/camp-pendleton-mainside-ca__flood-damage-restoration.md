## Why Camp Pendleton Mainside Properties See Flood Damage Issues

Camp Pendleton Mainside sits in the floodplain influence of the Santa Margarita River watershed, and heavy winter storm systems moving off the Pacific can push runoff faster than older storm drainage was designed to handle. Much of the housing stock on base is slab-on-grade, stucco-clad construction typical of mid-century and later Southern California military family housing, which means water intrusion tends to travel laterally under baseboards and through wall cavities rather than pooling visibly in one spot. Add in base roads and parking areas that were graded decades ago, and a heavy rain event can send sheet flow toward units that were never flooded before. ## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Camp Pendleton Mainside

Work on an active military installation starts with access. Every crew we send has to clear gate protocols and, depending on the housing area, coordinate an escort or sponsor check-in before equipment can roll onto the property, so we confirm those details with the resident or housing office before dispatch to avoid delay at the gate. Once on-site, extraction comes first with truck-mounted or portable pumps depending on water depth, followed by moisture mapping to find out how far water traveled under flooring and behind walls. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers placed and monitored daily until readings confirm the structure is dry, not just dry-looking. Any flooring, drywall, or insulation that absorbed contaminated water gets removed and documented before rebuild starts.

## Base Housing & Insurance Coordination

Flood damage inside privatized military family housing usually involves a different chain than a typical civilian claim. For mainside administrative buildings or commercial spaces, the process looks more like a standard commercial claim, and our team handles direct communication with the adjuster so the person managing the property isn't stuck relaying technical details back and forth.
